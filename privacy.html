Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the LifeKit Android application is designed to handle information. LifeKit is intended to provide everyday utility tools with a local-first approach.

1. No mandatory account

LifeKit does not require you to create an account, provide a phone number, or provide an email address just to use the app.

2. Local app data

Information you enter into supported LifeKit tools, such as notes, tasks and expenses, is intended to be stored locally on your device for the app's functionality.

3. Permissions

Some features may request Android permissions when you use them. Permissions should be limited to functionality that needs them, such as notifications or selecting local files. You can manage permissions from Android Settings.

4. Website

The LifeKit download website does not require an account to download the APK. The website itself is a static website and does not intentionally collect personal information through a LifeKit account system.

5. Third-party services

The website may load web resources such as fonts from third-party providers. Your browser may make requests to those providers as part of loading the website.

6. Children's privacy

LifeKit is a general-purpose utility application. We do not intentionally request personal information from children.

7. Changes

This policy may be updated when LifeKit's features or data practices change. The latest version will be published on this page.

8. Contact

If you later add a support email address, replace this paragraph with your official support contact before publishing a final commercial/privacy-policy version.

9. Important note

This policy describes the intended privacy design of the current LifeKit release. Before adding analytics, advertising, cloud accounts, payments, crash reporting or other external services, update this policy and the app's disclosures to match the actual implementation.
